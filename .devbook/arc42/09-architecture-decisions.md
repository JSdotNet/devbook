Technical choices taken and defensible. Records live in [`adr/`](adr/README.md), one per
concern, and are linked from here rather than restated, the same way
[chapter 11](11-risks-and-technical-debt.md) relates to [`tdr/`](tdr/README.md). The
[index](adr/README.md) lists every concern with its standing choice and says where the numbered
records that preceded them went.

A record that ends in options rather than a choice is debt, not a decision, and lives in
[chapter 11](11-risks-and-technical-debt.md) instead. A choice a reader could reverse with a
find-and-replace — a name, a layout, a process rule — is no record at all: the rule or chapter
that states it carries the reason.
